Privacy and control

Built around review.

What Inbox Tend reads, what it keeps, and what it never does.

Reads

After you connect Gmail with read-only consent, the app downloads your recent inbox and sent messages: headers, text and HTML bodies, labels and attachments up to a size limit. Gmail is contacted only to connect and when you press Refresh inbox.

Keeps

Everything it downloads is saved in a SQLite database on the server that runs Inbox Tend, together with the tasks you confirm. Only your Gmail refresh token is encrypted, so protect that server. Disconnecting Gmail deletes the saved messages and attachments.

Never does

No sending, deleting or trashing mail in Gmail, ever. Gmail access is read-only unless you choose to allow read/unread changes in Settings; then only the unread flag of one message at a time is changed. No AI model sees your messages unless you turn on the optional AI assistant in Settings; then only the one message you press an AI button on (or, if you also switch on Translation, each message you open, once, with the result saved here; or, if you switch on Sender details, each new sender once: name, address, timezone offset, up to two subjects and excerpts of up to two messages they sent you, about 1,000 characters each, to guess country, city, company, role, language, relationship and writing style; never gender, age, ethnicity, religion, health or politics) is sent to the provider you chose (OpenAI for Codex, Anthropic for Claude) through the command-line tool on the server, and results are only suggestions you review. Message bodies show as plain text only and attachments are never opened or run, only downloaded. Agent assignments only record a pending approval and run nothing.

Your controls

Disconnect or delete any time.

Disconnect Gmail in Settings → Gmail connection to remove the stored token and the saved mail. Deleting a task permanently removes it and its activity history. Public pages contain no inbox or task data.

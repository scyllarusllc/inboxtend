Self-hosted · open source · AGPL-3.0

Your mail, understood and turned into tasks.

Bring Gmail and IMAP mailboxes, including iCloud Mail, into one private workspace. Review messages, translate text, and turn follow-up into tracked tasks. Inbox Tend stores a local SQLite copy; it never sends or deletes mail from your mailbox.

Open workspace

Know who is writing

Every sender, at a glance

Mail is grouped as conversations, single messages or people. An optional AI guess, made once per sender and saved, adds a flag and a small card.

  • Country flag, city, company and role, language, relationship and writing style
  • Their local time now, and whether they are probably asleep
  • Your history together: how many emails, how fast they reply
  • Never gender, age or similar
  • Several Gmail accounts side by side, each with its own access

Know what it is about

Icons for money, work, love and more

Each message gets up to two round icons from 17 fixed topics. Press a topic on an open message, or search topic:money, to see everything about it.

  • Money, job, love, friends and family, travel, shopping, meetings, security, health, legal and more
  • One AI request per 20 messages, saved so a message is asked about once
  • Search like Gmail: from:, subject:, is:unread, has:attachment, topic:, instantly and locally

Read in your language

Offline translation next to the original

Turn on translation and an opened message appears in your language in a third column. Argos language packs run on your server; translation does not send mail to an AI provider. Results are saved in SQLite.

  • Install only the language directions you need
  • Choose from installed target languages in Settings
  • Off by default; the original message stays beside the translation

Get it done

Tasks with owners and deadlines

Review a suggested task, edit it, and save it with an owner, priority, deadline and status. For image-only mail, one suggestion copies the images to a task; translate and explain them on the Tasks page when you choose.

  • Deadlines in your timezone with a full audit history
  • Preview copied images and keep the AI review with the task
  • Reply drafts for you to review. Nothing is sent automatically

All your mailboxes

Gmail and IMAP in one workspace

Connect several Gmail accounts or an IMAP mailbox such as iCloud Mail. Synced messages are stored locally and can be searched together; narrow the Inbox to one account when you want to.

  • Gmail read/unread writes require your opt-in; IMAP read/unread changes update the server
  • Coloured markers show which account a message belongs to
  • Disconnecting an account removes its local mail copy, not mail from the provider

Anywhere

Works on your phone

The interface is responsive. Run it on a small server behind HTTPS and open it from any device.

Your data, your server

Private by design.

Mail lives in a SQLite file on your machine, behind a built-in login. Gmail read/unread writes require your opt-in. AI features are off by default, and image analysis runs only when you press its task button. No tracking, no CDN, no inline scripts. Read the privacy page.

For builders

One development server.

Run make dev to serve the pages and API together on 127.0.0.1:8765. Python changes restart the development server; page and asset changes refresh the browser. The backend uses FastAPI, SQLModel and Typer, with SQLite as the local store. Read the setup guide.

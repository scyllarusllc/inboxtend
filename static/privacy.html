Privacy and control

Built around review.

What Inbox Tend reads, what it keeps, and what it never does.

Reads

After you connect Gmail with read-only consent, the app shows your 20 most recent inbox messages: sender, subject, date and snippet. It does not fetch full bodies or attachments.

Keeps

Only the task fields you confirm, plus the Gmail message and thread IDs, are saved in a local SQLite database. Message content is fetched on demand and not stored. Your Gmail refresh token is stored encrypted.

Never does

No sending, deleting or modifying mail. No AI model sees your messages. Agent assignments only record a pending approval and run nothing.

Your controls

Disconnect or delete any time.

Disconnect Gmail from the Inbox page to remove the stored token. Deleting a task permanently removes it and its activity history. Public pages contain no inbox or task data.
